Kimi-Linear-48B-A3B: native 1M context scaling

Needle-in-a-haystack verification on a Mac Studio M3 Ultra, 512GB (mlx-lm 8-bit). Multi-needle retrieval (8 needles at 8 depths) stayed perfect 8/8 at every length with a truncation guard proving full-context ingestion. The cost is prefill time, which explodes with context because of the model's 7 full-attention MLA layers. The full ladder is now measured 2K to 1M: native 1M holds, with perfect recall at a real 1,048,692-token prompt and prefill (~5.0 h at 1M) the only steep price.
